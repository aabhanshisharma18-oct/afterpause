Contribution guidelines

Share work you can exhibit

Credit every original creator and source. Upload only material you have permission to share, and declare the terms of reuse on the exhibit. A public link does not grant reuse rights.

Offer a concrete next step

Say what you’ll try, what your first milestone is, and what materials you’ll use. Wait for the creator’s acceptance before continuing through Afterpause. Keep any license notices that apply to the original materials.

Show progress with credit

Send the creator a short update, screenshot, and optional external link. Approved updates appear under your name alongside the original creator. Ending or completing a continuation keeps approved work on the timeline. Archiving or moderation can hide the page while preserving those records.

Raise a concern

Use “Report attribution or content” on an exhibit to contact administrators privately. If your exhibit is hidden by moderation, your dashboard includes an appeal link.
